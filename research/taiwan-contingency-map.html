Taiwan Contingency

The atlas re-cut by the question that decides it: what matters when the shooting starts? The economic chokepoints run on a clock of years; a cross-strait fight runs on hours, weeks, and reload rates — which inverts the picture. The fast-biting, war-decisive capabilities mostly favor China, whose base is built for a short, high-intensity fight; the slow-biting economic chokepoints the US holds barely bite in time. The exceptions that keep it two-sided — undersea warfare, alliances, and the blockade's double edge on China's own imports — grow with time. An analytical exercise, not a forecast. who's advantaged:
The read: read the board left to right and the color shifts from red to blue. China's advantages are front-loaded — magazine depth, saturation, reload tempo, drone mass — the things that decide the opening fight. US and allied advantages — undersea, alliances, C2 quality, and the blockade turning China's own import dependencies against it — accumulate over months. The uncomfortable synthesis for a US planner: the longer the war, the more American advantages matter — and the whole point of China's industrial design is to make sure the war is short.
