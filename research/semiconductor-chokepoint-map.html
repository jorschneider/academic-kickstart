Semiconductor Chokepoint

The one domain where China still bends the knee — and is spending its way out. It cannot yet make a leading-edge chip the way TSMC does, because it cannot buy or build an EUV scanner. But it is brute-forcing near-frontier logic without EUV, localizing most of the rest of the toolchain, and locking up the mature nodes the whole world runs on. Each node below is coded by how self-sufficient China actually is. Tap a status to filter; click any node for its dossier.
