# PLA Purge Tracker, July 2023 – August 2026

Chronological record of senior PLA / People's Armed Police / defense-industry figures officially announced as under investigation, dismissed, stripped of NPC/CPPCC seats, expelled from the party/military, or sentenced — plus the *unannounced* disappearances that later resolved into confirmed removals. Compiled from NPC Standing Committee gazettes, MND statements, Xinhua, CCDI/military-court releases, and quality secondary reporting (SCMP, Caixin, Reuters, FT, Jamestown, CSIS, CMSI, PRC Leader/CLM, The Diplomat).

**Tags:** **[OFFICIAL]** PRC state announcement · **[REPORTED]** quality press/think-tank · **[INFERRED]** disappearance/replacement pattern · **[RUMOR]** émigré media only (Epoch Times, NTDTV, Secret China, Aboluowang, Kanzhongguo, Wenxuecity — used for leads, never as fact).

**Recurring official language:** 严重违纪违法 "serious violations of discipline and law"; 涉嫌严重职务犯罪，数额特别巨大 "suspected serious duty-related crimes, exceptionally large amounts"; 开除党籍/军籍 "expelled from party/military"; 移送军事检察机关 "referred to the military procuratorate." NPC removals run in two steps: recall (罢免) by the unit's military delegates' congress, then termination (资格终止) by NPCSC gazette.

## Pattern analysis

**Officially actioned senior figures by system** (counted once by primary affiliation):

| System | Count | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| Political-work / commissar system | **13** | Largest bloc: Miao Hua, He Hongjun, Bian Ruifeng, Qin Shutong, Yuan Huazhi, Qin Shengxiang, Yu Zhongfu, Guo Puxiao, Li Fengbiao, Yin Hongxing, Li Wei, Zhang Hongbing, Wang Renhua. The PWD ran the promotion market |
| Rocket Force | **8** (+3 reported) | 4 of its 5 commanders ever (Wei Fenghe, Zhou Yaning, Li Yuchao, Wang Houbin), a chief of staff (Sun Jinming), equipment/base chiefs, its own discipline secretary |
| Armaments/EDD + defense industry | **5 + 5** | Two consecutive EDD directors (Li Shangfu, Xu Xueqiang) + deputies; CASC/Norinco/CASIC/CALT/AVIC executives. Highest-rent portfolio class |
| Navy | **5** (+3 reported) | Two service PCs, an ex-commander, two fleet commanders — heavy at the political/HQ level |
| Air Force | **5** | Ex-commander (Ding Laihang), two ex-PCs, JLSF commander, a CTC deputy |
| Army HQ + theater ops | **9** | Li Qiaoming, You Haitao, Qin Shutong, Lin Xiangyang, Wang Xiubin, Ding Laifu, Wang Zhibin, Yin Hongxing, Liu Zhenli |
| CMC organs (JSD/LSD/T&AD/NDMD/Gen Office/Pol-Legal) | **7** | The connective tissue of the high command |
| SSF/ISF/Cyberspace | **4** | Every SSF commander the force ever had |
| PAP | **2** | Both top officers (Wang Chunning, Zhang Hongbing) |
| CMC principals | **5 of 6** uniformed 20th-CMC members | Only Zhang Shengmin untouched — and he was promoted |

**By network:** the **Oct 2025 nine** map to the 31st Group Army / Fujian / Miao Hua political machine (He Weidong, Miao Hua, Lin Xiangyang, Qin Shutong, Yuan Huazhi, He Hongjun + ETC subordinates). The **Jun 2026 six** map to the Zhang Youxia armaments/staff machine. The **Jan–Aug 2026 core** eliminated the princeling-patron pole (Zhang Youxia), the operational heir (Liu Zhenli), and Xi's own military secretary (Zhong Shaojun). *Caveat (Wuthnow, CLM 87): the "Fujian clique" frame is overdrawn — only ~2 Fujian natives besides Miao among the first 43 falls, ~4 with 31st GA service; readiness-driven housecleaning fits the aggregate better than any single-network story, with Zhang Youxia the main power-consolidation candidate.*

**A second removal channel.** Serving officers are stripped through the **NPC** (recall by a military delegates' congress, then an NPCSC gazette). **Retired** officers are stripped through the **CPPCC** — a channel this study initially under-weighted. The 2 March 2026 CPPCC session took Han Weiguo and Gao Jin together; Zhao Zongqi followed on 26 August 2026. Anyone tracking the purge by NPC gazettes alone will systematically miss the retired cohort, and the retired cohort is where the historical accountability is being settled.

**By year (senior official actions):** 2023 ≈ 12 · 2024 ≈ 8 · 2025 ≈ 19 · 2026 (to Aug) ≈ 23+. The cadence **accelerated**, climbed in rank each year, reached both *retirees* (Feb 2026) and *sitting corps commanders* (Ding Laifu), and in 2026 shifted from investigation announcements to **courtroom verdicts** (Tan Ruisong, Wei Fenghe, Li Shangfu — all suspended death sentences).

**Highest-risk portfolios revealed by the record:**
1. **Equipment/procurement** (EDD + service equipment departments + SOE primes) — three consecutive EDD leaderships, five industry chiefs.
2. **Political-work promotions pipeline** (PWD + service/theater PCs) — 13 falls; purging it invalidates a decade of vetting, which is why the wave self-propagates.
3. **Rocket Force / strategic-forces construction** — silo/base construction + solid-motor procurement; the Aug 2026 tip-solicitation signals it is not finished.
4. **Logistics/infrastructure** (LSD, JLSF, Training & Admin) — the classic Guo/Xu-era risk zone, still live.
5. **Internal-security commands** (PAP, Beijing/Central garrison lineage, CMC Political-Legal Commission) — loyalty-critical, zero-ambiguity posts.
