# Empirical Base Rates: what actually happens to PLA officers who reach the top

Companion to [`README.md`](README.md) and [`forecasting-model.md`](forecasting-model.md). The main report makes probabilistic claims; this file supplies the historical frequencies they are calibrated against. Everything here is constructed from official acts (Xinhua/MND statements, NPC Standing Committee gazettes, CPPCC Standing Committee decisions, military-court releases) and the leadership rosters that reproduce plenum communiqués, cross-checked against CSIS and *China Leadership Monitor*.

**A warning that governs the whole file:** these are historical frequencies over small, non-random, structurally interdependent populations. A Central Military Commission of six is not a sample. They discipline judgement; they do not generate forecasts for individuals. §5 says exactly what they cannot support.

Reaching the apex of the PLA has changed from a career capstone into approximately a coin flip, and lately worse than one.

## 3. The historical analogue: what happened to the last rebuild cohort

This is the most directly useful dataset in the study, because the PLA has run this experiment before. The Guo Boxiong / Xu Caihou purge (2012–2018) destroyed the top of the military and forced a near-total replacement of the high command between December 2015 and August 2017 — coinciding with the reform that abolished the four general departments and seven military regions. Those replacements have now had eight to ten years to be tested.

**Of the 28 distinct officers appointed to theater-command, service-headquarters, CMC-department or CMC-member level in that window, 14 were subsequently purged — exactly 50%.**

Broken down:

| Group | Purged | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Inaugural theater commanders (Feb 2016) | Zhao Zongqi, Han Weiguo | **2 of 5 = 40%** |
| Inaugural theater political commissars (Feb 2016) | — (one anomalous early retirement) | **0 of 5 = 0%** |
| Inaugural CMC department heads (Nov 2015) | Fang Fenghui, Zhang Yang, Zhang Youxia | **3 of 4 = 75%** |
| Inaugural service headquarters (Dec 2015) | Wei Fenghe, Gao Jin | **2 of 6 = 33%** |
| **The August 2017 replacement wave** | Miao Hua, Li Shangfu, Han Weiguo, Shen Jinlong, Qin Shengxiang, Ding Laihang, Yu Zhongfu, Zhou Yaning | **8 of 11 = 72.7%** |

The August 2017 wave is the tightest analogue to today's rebuild: officers promoted *directly into billets emptied by a purge*. Nearly three-quarters of them were themselves purged.

### 3.1 The duration effect — the most useful finding in this file

Within the 28-officer cohort, the split is stark:

- Officers who **left post by the end of 2018** (mostly late-career Jiang/Hu-era figures finishing out): **1 of 12 purged = 8.3%**
- Officers who **remained in senior post into the 2020s**: **13 of 16 purged = 81.3%**

**Being promoted after a purge was not itself dangerous. Still being in the promoted generation when the next wave arrived was.** Exposure duration, not the fact of post-purge elevation, predicts the outcome.

This inverts an intuition the main report was at risk of encoding. Early elevation into the current rebuild — Zhang Zheng, Zhu Chuansheng, Wang Gang, Zhang Shuguang, Yang Zhibin, Han Shengyan — is **not** evidence of durable favour. It starts the exposure clock. On this base rate, the officers most likely to be running the PLA in 2041 may be precisely those who are *not* prominent in 2027, because prominence in a purge cycle is a hazard rate, not a shield.

### 3.2 Cross-service transplantation does not confer immunity

The cleanest natural experiment in the record: in July 2023 the Rocket Force's command was replaced wholesale by outsiders — Wang Houbin from the Navy, Xu Xisheng from the Air Force — explicitly because they came from outside the compromised missile-force officer corps. **Both were gone within 27 months** (Wang expelled October 2025; Xu sidelined from about the same date without any announcement). Whatever the purge is selecting for, an outsider's clean hands are not sufficient to pass it.

### 3.3 Institutions with a 100% failure rate

- **Equipment Development Department directors:** Zhang Youxia (2015–17), Li Shangfu (2017–22), Xu Xueqiang (2022–25). All three purged. The post has been vacant since October 2025 and the department's political commissar billet since April 2019.
- **Strategic Support Force and successor commanders:** Gao Jin (2015–19), Li Fengbiao (2019–21), Ju Qiansheng (2021–24). All three purged, stripped in March, June and August 2026 respectively.

When every occupant of a chair since its creation has fallen, the chair is the finding.

## 4. Timing: the intervals

### 4.1 Disappearance → first official action (n = 12)

- **Median 168 days (~5.5 months)**; mean 202.5; range **15 to 942 days**; interquartile span ≈ 56–215 days.
- High-confidence subset, both endpoints documented (n = 6): **median 98.5 days (~3.2 months)**.

Selected measured cases:

| Officer | Last confirmed appearance | First official act | Lag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Miao Hua | routine to ~Nov 2024 | 28 Nov 2024 suspension announced | ~15 d |
| **Zhang Youxia** | **22 Dec 2025** — announced Xi's promotion orders at the CMC ceremony | 24 Jan 2026 investigation | **33 d** |
| Li Shangfu | 29 Aug 2023, China–Africa security forum | 24 Oct 2023 removal as Defence Minister | 56 d |
| Fang Fenghui | 21 Aug 2017, received Thai Supreme Commander | 9 Jan 2018 investigation | **141 d** — the only officially counted interval in the record |
| Guo Puxiao | absent from the Dec 2025 promotion ceremony | 26 Jun 2026 NPC seat stripped | ~195 d |
| He Weidong | 11 Mar 2025, NPC closing session | 17 Oct 2025 expulsion | 220 d |
| Zhong Shaojun | left NDU post Oct 2025 | 28 Aug 2026 NPC recall | ~300 d |
| **Ju Qiansheng** | 29 Jan 2024, NPC delegates' inspection | 28 Aug 2026 NPC seat stripped | **942 d** |

Two corrections this dataset forces on the wider literature:

1. **Zhang Youxia did not vanish in November 2025.** English-language sources state he "disappeared from public view after November 2025"; Xinhua's release of 22 December 2025 records him announcing Xi's promotion orders at the general-promotion ceremony, with Zhang Shengmin presiding. His true lag is **33 days** — the second-shortest in the record, indicating he was taken with almost no warning.
2. **The tail is very long.** Ju Qiansheng's 942 days shows that silence lasting years does not rule out an eventual announcement. This is the main reason [`forecasting-model.md`](forecasting-model.md) treats the Hu Zhongming anomaly as unresolved rather than as evidence he is safe.

### 4.2 Downstream

- **First official action → expulsion:** median ~264 days. *Zhang Youxia and Liu Zhenli were 216 days past their investigation announcement and still not expelled as of 31 August 2026.*
- **Expulsion → sentencing:** median ~394 days (Fang Fenghui 127 d; Guo Boxiong 361; Tan Ruisong 394; Wei Fenghe and Li Shangfu 679 each). **No member of the nine expelled on 17 October 2025 has been indicted or tried as of 31 August 2026** — ten months on.
- **Full cycle, disappearance → verdict:** Fang Fenghui ~18 months; Li Shangfu ~32 months. The process has roughly **doubled in length** between the Guo/Xu wave and the present one.

## 5. Tenure compression

| Population | n | Mean | Median |
|---|---|---|---|
| Theater commanders, Feb 2016 – Aug 2026 (completed) | 21 | 27.6 mo | **19.0 mo** |
| — appointed 2016–2019 | 9 | 37.6 mo | **46.0 mo** |
| — appointed 2020–2025 | 12 | 20.2 mo | **15.5 mo** |
| Service commanders post-2016 | 14 | 35.4 mo | 30.5 mo |
| CMC department directors post-2016 | 12 | 38.8 mo | 34.5 mo |
| 2023–24 appointees who have since fallen | 9 | 19.8 mo | **20.0 mo** |

**Theater-commander median tenure has fallen by two-thirds**, from about 46 months to about 16. The institutional design implies roughly five-year terms — Li Qiaoming's five years and one month at Northern Theater is the longest of the entire post-reform era, and it is an outlier by a factor of three against current practice.

The record also confirms two formal rules worth holding onto for the age arithmetic in [`forecasting-model.md`](forecasting-model.md) §2: **mandatory retirement at 65 for full-theater-leader grade** (Liu Yuejun, Zhao Zongqi, Song Puxuan and Han Weiguo all left at exactly 65), and a **ten-year maximum in deputy-theater-leader grade**.

## 6. What these numbers cannot support

- **A forward-looking probability for any named individual.** Small, interdependent populations; no independence between cases; the purge is a single correlated process, not 34 draws.
- **Anything about officers still standing.** Right-censoring is severe and asymmetric. Yang Zhibin, Han Shengyan, Wang Gang, Zhang Shuguang, Zhang Zheng and the rest **have not survived their exposure window — they are still inside it.** The 50% figure in §3 was only measurable because eight years elapsed. Applying it to the current cohort predicts their eventual fate only if the process is stationary, which is precisely what is in doubt.
- **A reliable disappearance distribution.** Eleven of twelve "last seen" dates are outside reconstructions from ceremonial absences, not documented facts; only Fang Fenghui's 141 days is officially counted. Absence has innocent explanations, and the 942-day outlier can swamp any central estimate.
- **Discrimination between motives.** Outcome coding cannot separate corruption clean-up from factional consolidation from a readiness drive. Wuthnow's four-explanation test favours readiness-driven housecleaning; CSIS treats the espionage allegations around Zhang Youxia as likely pretext rather than established fact. Nothing in these tables adjudicates that; see [`corruption-analysis.md`](corruption-analysis.md) §3 for the charge-language approach to the question.
- **A count of the true purge population.** These tables count *official acts only*, which are a floor. CSIS counts ~101 senior officers dismissed or missing against roughly 36 officially confirmed. **Every rate here is therefore conservative.**

## 7. Data flags

- **Zhao Keshi** was head of the CMC Logistic Support Department, never a theater commander; the five inaugural theater commanders were Liu Yuejun, Wang Jiaocheng, Zhao Zongqi, Song Puxuan and Han Weiguo. His reported purge is unconfirmed by any official act and is excluded from the counts.
- **Zhang Shengmin** appears in no purge list. He is the sole surviving 20th-CMC member and was promoted to vice chairman on 23 October 2025.
- **Newly surfaced official acts** not previously in this study's tracker: **Zhao Zongqi** (ex-Western Theater commander) CPPCC membership revoked 26 August 2026; **Han Weiguo** (ex-Ground Force commander) and **Gao Jin** (founding SSF commander) both stripped at the same CPPCC Standing Committee session on 2 March 2026. The CPPCC channel is a purge instrument this report had under-weighted relative to the NPC one — retired officers are removed through the CPPCC.
- **Low-confidence rows**, flagged rather than counted: Wang Jianping's judicial outcome; Liu Lei's status; Zhao Keshi; the December 2025 date for Xu Xueqiang's detention (single low-reliability outlet).
