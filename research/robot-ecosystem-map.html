Robot Ecosystem

China ships ~97% of the world's humanoid robots and owns most of the supply chain, the industrial base, and increasingly the software. Each node below is coded by China's competitive position — and the pattern is the story: China leads almost everywhere, and depends only at the hardest component tiers (top reducers, force/tactile sensors, AI compute). Tap a status to filter; click any node for its dossier. ◆PLA marks a documented military link.
