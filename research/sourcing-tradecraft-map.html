The Reading Room

Most English writing on China's defense industry runs on the same dozen sources — the Pentagon report, a few think-tank studies, the trade press, and each other. Estimates harden into facts through repetition rather than verification. This is the other half of the atlas's methodology: where the evidence actually comes from, and how to go get it yourself. Twenty-seven channels, most Chinese-language, most free, nearly all underused — plus three findings no amount of secondary reading will give you.

01

Three things only the primary sources tell you

Each of these took under an hour to establish from open, free, mostly Chinese-language material — and each changes something in the atlas.

Finding 01 · The vulnerability map is published

Beijing publishes its own chokepoint list

The "Little Giants" programme 专精特新"小巨人"企业 is a MIIT designation for SMEs that — in the ministry's own framing — have mastered technologies in key industrial links to fill supply-chain gaps. The 2018 criteria were concrete: ¥100–400m revenue, ≥10% profit growth, R&D staff above 15% of headcount, at least five innovation patents.

It was built explicitly around 卡脖子 ("stuck neck") chokepoint anxieties after the 2018 rupture. By December 2024, six batches covered roughly 14,600 firms, overshooting the 14th Five-Year Plan target of 10,000. Read structurally, that roster is the party-state's own assessment of where its supply chains are weakest — and it inverts the usual problem. Western analysts normally have to infer which obscure component makers matter; here the government ranked them and published the names.

Where it lands: the highest-yield starting point for extending the supplier map. Several firms already in the atlas — Western Superconducting among them — carry exactly this pedigree.

Finding 02 · Military-civil fusion, in appointment records

There is no transfer mechanism because the officials are the same people

"Military-civil fusion" is usually described as a process — technology crossing from a civilian sector to a military one through some channel. The institutional reality is blunter and sits in personnel records.

Shan Zhongde became a Vice Minister of MIIT — the ministry running the Little Giants programme and civilian industrial policy — in February 2024. In January 2025 he additionally became Administrator of the China National Space Administration, Director of the China Atomic Energy Authority, and Director of SASTIND, the State Administration for Science, Technology and Industry for National Defense. One official: civilian industrial policy, civil space, atomic energy, and defense science and industry. The pattern repeats locally — the Beijing bureau administering civilian robotics subsidies is headed by an official who concurrently directs the city's Defense Science and Technology Industry Office.

Where it lands: the atlas's "sanctions treadmill" throughline. The 1260H list keeps widening — 188 entities by June 2026 — and the usual reading is American overreach. The personnel evidence suggests something less comfortable: the category is unbounded because the architecture genuinely does not separate the two at the top.

Finding 03 · A date you can put in a calendar

The PLA publishes its shopping list twice a year

Since January 2015 the PLA has run an official weapons-and-equipment procurement portal, weain.mil.cn, established under the then General Armament Department and now sitting with the CMC Equipment Development Department. It carries procurement notices, requirement announcements and supplier registration.

The operationally useful part is the cadence: procurement information publishes on the first working day of every January and July. Access is tiered — visitor, registered, certified — so the open tier shows less than a Chinese contractor sees, but it is not empty. SCMP derived reporting on a prospective airborne laser weapon programme directly from notices on the site.

Why it is open at all: the portal exists to widen the supplier pool and cut procurement barriers. That is the shape of the best Chinese sources — open because opacity would defeat their domestic purpose.

04

Five tradecraft rules

How to use these without getting fooled.

Prefer the physical to the declared

A teardown, a satellite image, a customs record and a shipped tonne are harder to spin than a capacity announcement. Every threshold in the atlas's tripwires is stated as a physical quantity for exactly this reason.

Personnel appointments are data

Who holds which post — and how many at once — often reveals institutional intent more reliably than any policy document. Finding 02 took ten minutes to verify and reframes a throughline.

Read what the state says to itself

Five-Year Plan implementation documents, procurement notices and military standards are written for domestic execution. White papers and spokespeople are written for you.

A paper is interest, not capability

The commonest failure in technical-literature work is treating a CNKI hit as evidence of a fielded system. It is evidence that someone was funded to think about it.

An absent source can be the finding

No public figure exists for Chinese munitions output. That void is why the ground-systems comparison is epistemically lopsided — and saying so beats importing a number from a secondary source that invented one.
