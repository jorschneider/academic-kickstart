The cost-exchange problem — why directed energy, why now

The bad trade
$400 FPV vs. $2M missile

Win the engagement, lose the economics. A saturation swarm of cheap drones bankrupts a missile-based defense — the core logic driving every choice on this board.

The answer China is racing on
~$1/shot · unlimited magazine

Lasers and high-power microwave (LY-1, Silent Hunter, Hurricane 3000): near-zero cost-per-shot and a magazine limited only by power — purpose-built for swarms.

The live gap
Fiber-optic drones

FO-guided FPVs emit no RF — they defeat the detect-by-RF and jam-by-RF layers at once, forcing the whole field toward radar, optical, and hard-kill.

The business
Arming both sides

China sells the drones (Wing Loong-3, ~$5B Saudi deal) and the counter-drones (Silent Hunter to Saudi/UAE/Russia). Both ship from one industrial base.
