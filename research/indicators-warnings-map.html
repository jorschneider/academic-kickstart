Tripwires

An atlas that cannot be wrong is not analysis — it is decoration. The scorecard says where China stands; the confidence grades say how much to trust it. These are the specific, observable events that would force a rating to change — each one naming a claim, the thing to watch, where it would surface, and what it would mean. Commitments made in advance, so the atlas can be checked against events rather than re-narrated after them.

A ChinaTalk research atlas · 25 tripwires across 14 domains · Compiled from open sources · Updated 31 Aug 2026

01

The asymmetry is itself a finding

Compile the tripwires honestly and a lopsidedness appears immediately: three in five point toward China being stronger than rated, not weaker. That is not a thumb on the scale — it reflects the evidence environment. Chinese capability tends to be revealed late and in one direction: the J-36 flew before its configuration was known, two operational XLUUV classes rolled down a parade route drawn from inventory rather than prototypes. Open-source analysis of an opaque system is biased toward understatement, and the corrections mostly correct upward.

Width = share of tripwires. The constraining signals are real, but slower, more diffuse, and heavily covered from a Western vantage point — easy to over-read.

The most valuable indicators are not the ones that would nudge a rating — they are the ones that would tell us the map was drawn wrong.

On sourcing

Each tripwire names a claim, an observable, a source, and a consequence, so the atlas commits itself in advance. Confidence letters (A well-documented · B mixed evidence · C largely inferential) come from the companion methodology-and-confidence.md. Where an indicator rests on contested reporting — the December 2025 EUV prototype above all, where coverage ranged from credible specialist analysis to content-farm noise — the dossier says so rather than laundering it into fact. Thresholds are stated as physical quantities (watts, tonnes shipped, satellites per month) precisely so they cannot be satisfied by an announcement. This is a standing commitment, not a forecast.
